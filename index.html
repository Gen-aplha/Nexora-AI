What is NEXORA AI?

NEXORA AI is an online AI assistant designed to make it easier to explore questions, develop ideas and get help with everyday creative and learning tasks. You can start a conversation, revisit saved chats and try supported creative tools from one simple interface.

Created with curiosity

NEXORA AI was created by Mayank Gupta, a 14-year-old school student interested in technology and building useful digital projects.

Learn more about the project and its creator on the About page, explore the features, or open the AI chat assistant.
