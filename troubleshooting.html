Android troubleshooting guide

Having trouble with NEXORA AI?

If the app freezes, stops responding, or shows an unexpected error, try these steps first.

1

Open the app information screen

Find the NEXORA AI icon on your Android home screen or app list. Touch and hold the icon, then tap App info (ⓘ). On some phones, you may need to tap the small information icon.

2

Force stop NEXORA AI

On the App info screen, tap Force stop. If Android asks you to confirm, tap OK or Force stop again.

3

Open the app again

Return to your home screen or app list and tap NEXORA AI to launch it again. Check whether the issue has disappeared.

4

If the problem continues

Check your internet connection, then close and reopen NEXORA AI once more. If you use NEXORA AI in a browser, refresh the chat page or close and reopen the browser.

Important: Force stop only closes the app; it does not uninstall it. Do not tap Clear storage, Clear data or Uninstall as part of this troubleshooting step. If you have unsent text, copy it before closing the app.

Still seeing the error?

Report the issue to the NEXORA AI project. Include what you were doing and the exact error message, but do not include passwords or private account information.
