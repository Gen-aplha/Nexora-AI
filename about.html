About the project

Built around curiosity.

NEXORA AI is an independent AI chat project designed to make exploring questions and ideas feel simple and approachable.

Our goal

We want to give people a clear, welcoming place to ask questions, learn new concepts, draft ideas and get unstuck. The website is designed to work on mobile screens as well as desktop browsers.

What NEXORA AI can do

Depending on the connected AI service, it can help with explanations, brainstorming, writing, planning and code-related questions. Some features, such as voice input, depend on the browser and device.

Responsible use

AI can make mistakes and may produce outdated or incomplete answers. Verify important information, especially for health, legal, financial or safety decisions. Do not submit passwords, payment details or other sensitive personal information.

Privacy note

The chat interface saves conversation history in local browser storage on the device and browser where it is used. Messages are also sent to the configured NEXORA AI backend to generate replies. Review the backend provider's privacy practices before publishing this website widely, and add a clear privacy policy/contact address before collecting user data.

Contact

Contact details have not been added to this starter website. Add an email address you control before launch if you want visitors to contact the project owner.
