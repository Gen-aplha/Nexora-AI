NEXORA AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Welcome to NEXORA AI. Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how information is handled when you visit our website or use our AI assistant.

1. Information You Provide

When using NEXORA AI, you may provide text messages, questions, images, documents or other content. Please avoid sharing passwords, financial details, private documents or other sensitive information.

2. Chat History and Local Storage

NEXORA AI may save chat history and preferences in your browser's local storage or IndexedDB. This information is stored on your device and may remain there until you delete it or clear your browser data.

3. AI Processing

Messages and supported attachments are sent to our AI backend hosted using Cloudflare Workers. Cloudflare Workers AI processes requests to provide chat responses, image analysis and image generation.

Your content is processed to provide the feature you requested. Cloudflare's own privacy and data handling practices may also apply.

4. Uploaded Files and Images

When you upload a supported image or document, its content may be sent to our backend for AI processing. Only upload files you have permission to share.

5. Voice Features

Voice input and spoken responses may use speech recognition and speech synthesis features provided by your browser or device. Their privacy practices may apply separately.

6. Third-Party Services

NEXORA AI uses third-party services, including GitHub Pages for website hosting and Cloudflare for backend and AI processing. These providers may process technical information according to their own policies.

7. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable measures to protect NEXORA AI. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Please do not share confidential information.

8. Children's Privacy

NEXORA AI is not intended to encourage children to share personal or sensitive information. Users should follow applicable age requirements and seek a parent or guardian's guidance where appropriate.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as NEXORA AI gains new features or changes how it operates. Updates will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or concerns, contact the NEXORA AI project through its GitHub Issues page .
