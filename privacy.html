NEXORA AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy explains how NEXORA AI handles information when you use its website, AI chat, account features and collaboration tools. NEXORA AI is an independent project. Do not submit passwords, payment details, government identifiers, private records or information about another person unless you have permission and it is necessary.

1. Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, you may submit chat messages, prompts, uploaded images or documents, feedback, profile details, account email information, workspace messages, invitation details and files shared with a team. Please keep personal and sensitive information out of prompts and shared workspaces.

2. Local storage and browser data

Some chat history, preferences, reminders and other app data may be stored in your browser or device using local storage or IndexedDB. This data can remain on that device until you clear it or use an in-app deletion feature. Browser or app removal may affect locally stored data.

3. Accounts and cloud synchronisation

Google sign-in and cloud chat synchronisation use Supabase Auth and Supabase database services. Account-related information and synced chat data may be stored in the NEXORA AI Supabase project. NEXORA Team Space uses Supabase to manage workspaces, memberships, invitations, conversations and shared messages. Team members who have access to a workspace may see its shared content.

NEXORA AI also has a separate email-and-password account backend using Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare D1. Accounts in that system are distinct from Google/Supabase accounts unless the app explicitly provides a linking feature. Do not assume signing into one system signs you into the other.

4. AI processing and third-party providers

When you send a prompt or request an AI feature, the relevant content is sent to NEXORA AI backend services hosted on Cloudflare Workers. AI requests are processed using Cloudflare Workers AI. Some search and web-summary functions may use an external search provider when configured. The relevant provider may process request content and technical data under its own terms and privacy policy.

5. Uploaded images and documents

Files may be processed by the AI backend or browser-based tools to provide the feature you selected. Team Space files are stored in a private Supabase Storage bucket and are intended to be accessible to authorised workspace members. Do not upload files you do not own or have permission to share.

6. Voice and translation features

Voice input, live translation and spoken output may use speech recognition, text-to-speech or OCR libraries provided by your browser or loaded from third-party content delivery networks. Your device, browser and those providers may process audio, image or text data according to their own policies. Microphone access is controlled by your browser or device.

7. Notifications and reminders

Smart Notifications reminders are currently stored locally on the device/browser. Browser notifications require permission. Timely alerts may not occur when the app or browser is fully closed; this feature is not a guaranteed background or emergency notification service.

8. Security, access and retention

NEXORA AI uses access controls and service-provider security features, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Data may remain in provider backups, operational logs or account records according to provider retention practices and project configuration. Do not treat NEXORA AI as a secure vault or submit confidential information.

9. Your choices and deletion

You can delete locally stored chats or reminders using the available app controls and clear browser storage from your device settings. Cloud-synced account data and Team Space content may require deletion through the relevant account or workspace controls. For a data-access or deletion request that cannot be completed in the app, contact the project through GitHub Issues. Do not include passwords or private account credentials in a request.

10. Children's privacy

NEXORA AI is not designed to encourage children to disclose personal information. Younger users should use the service with appropriate parent or guardian guidance and follow the applicable service-provider age requirements.

11. Changes and contact

This policy may change as features and providers change. Updates will be posted on this page. For questions or privacy requests, use the NEXORA AI GitHub Issues page.
